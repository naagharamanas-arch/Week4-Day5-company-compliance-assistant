SAMPLE COMPANY DATA PRIVACY POLICY

Policy ID: PRIV-001
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This policy explains how employees must handle company and customer data.

2. Customer Data Protection
Employees must protect customer personal information and access it only for approved business purposes.

3. Sharing Customer Data
Employees must not share customer personal information with external vendors unless the vendor has been approved and the required data protection agreement is in place.

4. Data Storage
Customer data must be stored only in company-approved systems. Employees must not upload customer information to personal cloud storage.

5. Reporting Incidents
Employees must report suspected data leaks or unauthorized access to the company's security team immediately.

6. Missing Policy Information
If a question cannot be answered using this policy, the assistant must state that the available policy does not contain sufficient information. It must not invent company rules.

Note: This is a fictional sample policy created for a learning project. It is not a real company's official policy.
